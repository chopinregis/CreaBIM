# BIM words explained

**Area**: a part of the window showing one view (the 3D view, a plan, a sheet…) under its own header. See [View areas](interface#view-areas).

**Assembly**: a named list of layers (material, thickness, function) that walls, floors or roofs are built of. See [Layered assemblies](assemblies).

**BIM**: Building Information Modelling. Modelling a building as real elements that know what they are, rather than as lines.

**Core**: the structural layers of a layered wall, floor or roof.

**Crop box**: a box that cuts a 3D view down to what is inside it. See [Crop boxes](crop-boxes).

**Curtain wall**: a wall made of a grid of mullions holding glass or panels. See [Curtain walls](curtain-walls).

**Cut height**: how high above its level a floor plan cuts the building.

**Edit mode**: an element opened so every one of its points, edges and faces can be picked and reshaped (`Tab`). See [Edit mode](edit-mode).

**Element**: one thing in the model: a wall, a door, a slab, a level, a view.

**Elevation**: a level's height above the project's zero, or a view of the building's outside from one side. See [Sections and elevations](sections).

**Extrude**: pull a flat shape out into a solid.

**Grid**: labelled reference lines (A, B, C and 1, 2, 3) for setting out a building. See [Grids](grids).

**Hatch**: the pattern drawn where a section cuts a material.

**Host**: the element another belongs to: a wall hosts its doors and windows.

**IFC**: Industry Foundation Classes, the open file format BIM programs share models with. See [IFC](ifc).

**Insertion point**: where a profile sits on the path it is swept along. See [Profiles](profiles).

**Instance**: one element of a [type](types).

**Level**: a floor of the building at a height. Also called a storey. See [Levels](levels).

**Location line**: the line a wall is drawn and dimensioned on. See [Walls](walls#location-line).

**Mass**: a free 3D shape, for studies or anything that isn't a standard element. See [Masses](masses).

**Mullion**: a bar of a curtain wall's grid.

**Override**: a value an instance sets for itself instead of taking its type's.

**Pitch**: the slope of a roof, in degrees.

**Plan**: a view from above, cut at the cut height. See [Views](views).

**Profile**: a cross-section drawn once and used for sweeps, extrusions, columns, beams and railings. See [Profiles](profiles).

**Revision cloud**: a scalloped outline on a sheet round what changed, with its revision letter. See [Sheets](sheets#revisions).

**Schedule**: a table of elements made from the model. See [Schedules](schedules).

**Section**: a view of the building cut along a line. See [Sections and elevations](sections).

**Shaft**: a vertical hole through the floors between two levels. See [Holes](holes#shafts).

**Sheet**: a page of paper with views placed on it and a title block. See [Sheets](sheets).

**Sill**: the height of a window's (or opening's) bottom edge above its wall's base.

**Slab**: a floor, ceiling or terrace plate. See [Slabs](slabs).

**Snap**: the pointer jumping to an exact point, such as a corner. See [Snaps](snaps).

**Style pack**: a project's drawing standard in one file: pens, object styles, hatches, view templates and number formats. See [Graphic styles](graphic-styles).

**Type**: a shared definition many elements follow. See [Types](types).

**Underlay**: another level shown faintly under a plan, to trace.

**View**: a saved way of looking at the model: plan, section, elevation, 3D view or schedule.

**Viewport**: a view placed on a sheet.

**Workplane**: the plane sketches and guides drawn in 3D go on. See [Sketches](sketches#the-workplane).

**Workspace**: a named layout of view areas: *Model*, *Drawing*, *Document*, *Sheets* or your own. See [Workspaces](interface#workspaces).
