# Step 2: set up the levels

A **level** is a floor of the building: the ground floor, the first floor, the roof. It has a height (its *elevation*, such as +3000 mm). Walls, slabs and everything else stand on a level, so when a level moves, what is on it moves too.

## Add a level

1. Open the Levels window: *View › Levels…*, or the **⋯** button on the *Levels* group in the project browser on the left.
2. Click **+ New**. A new level appears one storey above the highest, with its own floor plan. (*Create › Views and levels › Level above the top one* does the same from anywhere.)
3. Click a level's name to rename it, and type its elevation to change its height.

![The Levels window on the starter house: the levels down the left, the picked level's settings, Add a level, and + New at the foot](img/levels.jpg)

The **+** on the *Levels* group, or *Create › Views and levels › Level above the top one*, adds a level in one click.

## Work on a level

Open a level's floor plan from the project browser (under *Floor plans*) or from an area's header (its view-type menu, then the plan's name). New walls and floors go on the level of the plan you draw in.
