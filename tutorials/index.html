Tutorials

Learn it by building.

Start with a first house in eight short steps, then dip into the guide for any tool. Every page here is also inside the app, one key away: F1.

01Getting started

A first house, step by step.

A small one-storey house from scratch, ending with a printed sheet and an IFC file. Each step takes a few minutes; stop at any point and come back.

  1. 1Start a projecta new empty project, or a sample to look at
  2. 2Set up the levelsthe floors of the building
  3. 3Draw the wallsthe outline of the house
  4. 4Add doors and windowsthey cut their own holes
  5. 5Add a floor and a roofslabs and roofs from an outline
  6. 6Look at it: plans, sections and 3Dviews made from the model
  7. 7Put it on a sheeta drawing ready to print
  8. 8Save and exportthe project file, PDF and IFC

Want to see a finished model first? CreaBIM opens on the starter house, and File › New starter house brings it back.
