# CreaBIM 1.1.0

Curved walls, a Masters view for title blocks, beams on any face, pictures on sheets, and a calmer, faster window.

## Walls

- **Arc, circle and rectangle walls.** The Wall tool draws straight walls, arcs (start, end, a point it passes through), circles (centre, then radius) and rectangles (two corners, four joined walls). The ghost shows the real shape, in plans and in 3D, before you click.
- **Curved walls work like straight ones.** They carry layers, doors and windows (standing on the arc), curtain grids (flat panels round the curve), strip footings, rooms and slabs that fill to them, and clean joins where they meet other walls.
- **Bend a curved wall afterwards** with the grip at its middle, or type its radius in Properties.
- Sections and elevations draw a curved wall's outline only, without facet lines.

## Beams, columns and railings

- **Beams on faces:** draw a beam on any face in 3D, sloped or level; it sits on the face and goes on the level under it.
- Beam chains with **mitred, butt or separate corners**, rises, extensions and cuts.
- Columns, beams and railings take a **profile** from the library or your own.
- Plans tint every member seen from above, cut where the plan plane crosses it; columns rising from the level below show in projection.

## Sheets

- **Masters view** for title blocks: lines, rectangles, circles, ellipses, text, fields (project, sheet, date, your own), guides, pictures and SVG logos kept as crisp vectors; snapping, opacity, order and margins.
- **Pictures on sheets:** any number of PNG, JPEG or SVG images, sized by their grips, with their own opacity.

## Modelling

- Modifiers for architecture (arrays straight, radial and along a path, bevel, shell, slice, panelize, louvres, taper, twist, mirror, solidify, subdivision) with a richer panel; Divide by count or size from a bar over the views.
- Split, Inset and a slimmer Move bar; modifier guides while you edit.
- Edit mode picks as Blender's (vertices, edges, faces); arcs in profiles; a Rectangle shape in sketches.
- The placing ghost shows in every view, not only the one you work in.
- Roof Properties say where the eaves end, and warn when a steep pitch and a long overhang hang them low enough to hide the walls.

## Working in the app

- **Closing with unsaved changes asks** Save, Don't save or Cancel.
- **Log view and Help › Report a problem:** one file with what the app did lately, the slow steps, your settings, your system and graphics card, and your project only if you tick it. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Settings laid out as Blender's Preferences, with a selection colour per theme; the Solarized theme.
- Select tools in the toolbar, a live box-select count, mouse hints in the status bar, Ctrl+middle-drag zoom.
- Properties fit narrow panels; view controls stay usable in short areas.

## Faster

- Drags in 3D no longer rebuild every plan on each frame; the project tree holds still during a drag.
- The window keeps priority over background drawing builds, so a busy machine stays responsive.
- Finer timing in the activity log, to find what is slow.

## Known gaps

See [KNOWN_GAPS.md](../KNOWN_GAPS.md). Crossing curved walls are not yet cleaned up where they overlap, and curved curtain walls export to IFC straight.
