# CreaBIM 1.2.0

The site, every door, window and stair, phasing, a render with the sun and the lamps, project time against the fee, and drawings with a hand of their own.

## Starting a project

![The New project window, its parts numbered](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/v1.2.0/crates/creabim-app/help/img/start-window.jpg)

- **A New project window:**
  - **Start:** an empty project, the new starter house, or the starter of an earlier version.
  - **Templates:** ten sets, each with its sheets and title blocks ready: Studio, Ledger, Field Book, Monograph, Blueprint, Workshop, Gallery, Interior, Sketchbook and Series.
  - **My templates:** your own, made from any project.
  - **Samples:** six small projects to learn from, each showing one thing, from a simple house to a house in its garden.
- **A drawing standard per project.** *ISO* gives A sheets and ratio scales. *US* gives ARCH and ANSI sheets, architect's scales from 12" = 1'-0" to 1/16" = 1'-0", engineer's scales, and sets bound in discipline order (G, C, L, S, A…).
- **Ridgeline House** is the new starter: a timber house on a sloping lot, from the site survey to a permit set. Maple Lane House, the 1.1 starter, is still there.
- **Imperial projects** start with imperial door and window sizes.

In the guide: [Start a project](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/start-project.html).

## The site

- **Terrain and earthworks:**
  - ground, contour lines, spot levels and property lines;
  - pads, pits and trenches;
  - roads and paths;
  - paving, driveways, gravel, decking and asphalt;
  - drains and tanks.
- **Bring in a survey:**
  - a table of points, from Excel, `.csv`, `.txt`, or rows pasted from a spreadsheet;
  - a DXF drawing (spot heights, contours, break lines);
  - LandXML, GeoTIFF, Esri ASCII grids, GeoJSON or a shapefile.

  Codes surveyed as lines (a kerb, the top of a bank) become break lines, so the ground folds along them.
- **The soils report in the model:** boreholes, trial pits and probes, with an *Investigation* site view of the ground as surveyed.
- **Planting:** trees, conifers, palms and shrubs. In plan each is a symbol as wide as its canopy. In 3D it grows, standing on the ground under it.

In the guide: [Site](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/site.html), [Planting and trees](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/planting.html).

## Doors and windows

- **17 kinds of door and 14 kinds of window**, each built as it is really made:
  - hinged, double, sliding (on the face or in a pocket), multi-slide, pivot, two-way, bifold, folding, sectional, roller and revolving doors;
  - fixed, casement, awning, hopper, tilt-turn, sliding, hung, pivot, louvred and roof windows.
- **Drawn the way an architect expects:**
  - in plan with their swings;
  - in elevation and section with opening triangles;
  - in schedules with their operation;
  - in IFC with their operation type.
- **One door, many leaves:** flush, panelled, glazed, louvred, French, slim steel or all glass. A window can have glazing bars, sidelights, a transom or an arched head.
- **A picker with pictures**, before you place one or to change one already placed. *Make a type* names a kind with its sizes and reuses it.

In the guide: [Doors and windows](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/doors-windows.html).

## Stairs

- **Drawn along a walk line**, with a landing or winders at each turn: straight, L, U, curved and spiral.
- **Built the way stairs are built:** concrete (flat or stepped underside), folded plate, stringers, cut stringers, a central spine, cantilevered treads, or a spiral round a post. Tread and riser finishes, and nosings.
- **Rules and checks:**
  - stair rules (Comfort, US, UK) checked in a live schedule;
  - *Check stairs*, on the *Manage* tab, selects every stair that fails a check;
  - headroom is measured along the walk line, and **Cut a well** opens the floor above.

In the guide: [Stairs and railings](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/stairs-railings.html).

## Phasing

![One plan at Existing, Demolition and Proposed](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/v1.2.0/crates/creabim-app/help/img/phases-plans.jpg)

## Render and light

![The starter house in every look, from hidden line to photoreal](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/v1.2.0/crates/creabim-app/help/img/render-looks.jpg)

- **Every look in the 3D view:** hidden line, wireframe, shaded, toon, clay, line and wash, watercolour, sketch, quick, quick with reflections, and photoreal.
- **Moods:** Day, Morning, Evening, Overcast and Night.
- **The sun moves through the day.** The model's own light fittings light the rendered looks, each group with its own strength.
- **Render and keep:** a render is made in the background and kept in the project, in the *Renders* area.

In the guide: [Render and light](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/render.html).

## Drawings

- **Line styles:** a drawing's lines can be ruled, or drawn by hand. The hand styles are Hand, Loose, Vellum Graphite, Linen Ink, Marker & Wash, Field Bold and Millwork Study. They draw in plans, sections and elevations, on sheets and in PDF and SVG.
- **Pen sets:** four ship with CreaBIM, Technical, Sketch, Presentation and Fine line, each a pen table by scale.
- **The palette:** seven ship with CreaBIM: Ink, Sepia, Olive, Terracotta, Blueprint, Lavender and coral, and Material. You can also make your own from a few settings. It recolours every drawing, and the preview shows how a colour-vision difference sees it.
- **Object styles:** how each category draws, cut and in projection.
- **Symbols:** a library of 150, 15 kinds of mark in ten styles each.
- **Grids:**
  - a system can be turned to its own north, or curved into rings and spokes;
  - a line can bow into an arc, show only on some levels, or be drawn shorter in one plan;
  - crowded bubbles step aside.
- **Dimensions:** words before, after and below the number, and text you can move, with a leader back.
- **Ceiling plans:** a plan's View range can look up.

In the guide: [Graphic styles](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/graphic-styles.html), [Grids](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/grids.html), [Dimensions](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/dimensions.html), [View range](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/view-range.html).

## Notes and sheets

![A sheet with a revision cloud, a revisions table and a key plan](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/v1.2.0/crates/creabim-app/help/img/sheets-marks.jpg)

- **The Notes area:** general, structural, electrical and plumbing notes, and your own, written in Markdown with fields that fill themselves (`{project}`, `{address}`, `{issue_date}`…). Drafts and to-dos are kept apart.
- **Notes on sheets stay linked:** edit the section and every sheet that shows it updates.
- **Long notes, broken your way:** give a block a height, then *Continue…*. The text flows on into the next column, and on again as you edit.
- **Notes that read an object:** a note can show an element's name, mark, type, material, level, size or layers, and follow its arrow onto another element.
- **The Sheet tab:** a placed view's title, number and scale.
- **North arrows** turn to true north. **Scale bars** read the drawing they measure.
- **Revision clouds** carry their letter.
- **A key plan** is placed over the title block.
- **The same mark on many sheets** from one *Placed on* list.

In the guide: [Notes](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/notes.html), [Sheets and title blocks](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/sheets.html).

## IFC

- **The Data tab** shows an element's GlobalId, class, storey, property sets and quantities, and what the export writes.
- **Data read from IFC is kept:** property sets and classifications go back out on export.
- **Documents per element:** data sheets, specs and links, each opened with one click.
- **Declared ratings** (fire, acoustic, U-value) write the common property sets.
- **Assign as** turns a shape or an imported element into a wall, slab, door or window.
- **Groups** a file brings in are kept, listed in the browser and written back out. Groups that hold design options can become options.

In the guide: [IFC](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/ifc.html).

## Project time

- **The hours of each work stage against its fee and hours budget**, so you see early when a stage runs over.
- It counts working time while CreaBIM is in front, and stops when you're idle. Add manual entries for site visits and meetings.
- **It is off until you switch it on** for a project. It stays on your own computer and is never sent anywhere.

In the guide: [Project time](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/project-time.html).

## Working in the app

- **Tool families:** one button per family, with the last tool used. The arrow opens the whole family.
- **Draw by:** the same outline choices for every tool that draws one (polygon, rectangle, fill room, from sketch).
- **The Select tab:** what you picked by category, *Select similar* by type, category or values, and *All of* a kind.
- **Stretch** (`Shift+S`) and **Corner** (`J`).
- **Properties start with the same Element rows for everything**, and give each subject its own tab.
- **Interface scale:** *Settings › Interface › Look › Interface scale* makes the whole window larger or smaller.
- **The mouse wheel:** *Automatic* zooms with a mouse wheel and pans with two fingers on a touchpad.
- **Where you come from** (*experimental*): pick Revit, ArchiCAD, AutoCAD, SketchUp or Blender, and the keys follow what your hands already know. It won't match those programs one to one, and it will be refined over time.
- **Duplicate view** (`Shift+D`, or its button) makes the copy at once.
- **On macOS, the Mac's own menu bar:** CreaBIM's menus are in the menu bar at the top of the screen, with their `⌘` shortcuts, and the window has the standard macOS buttons beside the tool bar.

In the guide: [The window at a glance](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/interface.html), [Selecting](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/select.html).

## Fixes

- **Grid lines no longer drift** ([#81](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/81)).
- **The wheel zooms** instead of switching to pan ([#78](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/78)).
- **Guides take a typed length** ([#34](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/34)). Type it from a first point, or, from a wall or guide, type how far across the new guide goes.
- **Joins keep their faces** where walls meet slabs.
- **Save As on macOS** opens as a sheet on the project's window.
- **3D highlights** no longer show a face's inner diagonals.
- **DXF import:**
  - explodes blocks and dimensions;
  - reads files that aren't UTF-8;
  - stops at a million items, and says so.
- **Masters** keep what you type in a text's words, its fields and a master's name.
- **Bar tooltips** no longer stay over the drawing.
- **Hatch lines along a mitred corner** draw the same on every system.
- **Fixed since 1.1.1, and first released here:**
  - a crash when pointing at a door, window or furniture in 3D ([#22](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/22));
  - export names keep the dots in a project's name;
  - the New project window fits a short screen ([#24](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/24));
  - no console window beside CreaBIM;
  - the redesigned *Save the changes* card.

  See [1.1.2](1.1.2.md).

## Windows setup

- **A standard installer** ([#53](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/53)). The setup is built with Inno Setup and runs no PowerShell when it installs or uninstalls. It still needs no administrator rights.
- **Not code-signed yet.** If Windows says the publisher is unknown, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*. Check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
