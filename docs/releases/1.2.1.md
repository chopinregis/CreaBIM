# CreaBIM 1.2.1

Fixes to 1.2.0: CreaBIM starts at any interface scale, menus are in solid panels again, and the working plan draws in the project's styles, as its sheets do.

## Starting CreaBIM

- **No more crash at start-up** when the interface scale is saved at anything but 100 %. Reported on Windows and macOS ([#131](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/pull/131)).
- **CreaBIM says why it can't start**, instead of closing without a word ([#31](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/31)).
- **The New project window fits** between the bars at every interface scale.

## Menus and panels

- **Menus, cards and tips are in solid panels again** ([#112](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/112), [#121](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/121)).
- **At 125 % scaling,** menus and cards keep their right and bottom edges in the window.
- **Catalogue:** the search takes one row, not half the window ([#113](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/113)).

## Drawing and modelling

- **The working plan draws the project's object styles,** their colours and line patterns, as its sheets do.
- **Cut faces › Fill** is painted behind the hatch in the working plan, as on sheets. Plain projects look the same as before.
- **Rooms:** a room separator drawn into a wall's end closes the room, and so does one along the walls' centre line across a doorway.
- **Snaps** put the grip itself on the target when you drag a slab's grips.
- **Notes:** `{Size}` reads a door's or window's width × height ([discussion #57](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/discussions/57)).

In the guide: [Rooms](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/rooms.html), [Notes](https://chopinregis.github.io/CreaBIM/guide/1.2/notes.html).

## Command line

- `creabim --help` lists the `styled` command (part of [#129](https://github.com/chopinregis/CreaBIM/issues/129)).

## Thanks

To everyone who reported these, here and on the OSArch forum, and for the pull request that found the start-up crash on macOS.
